# 🚀 Welcome to Blog App

Share your ideas and discover amazing content from the community.

## What is Blog App?

Blog App is a platform where users can:

- **Create and share** their own blogs
- **Discover content** from other users
- **Manage a personal** reading list
- **Like** their favorite posts

## Main Features

### 📝 Create Blogs
Share your knowledge, experiences, and ideas with the community.

### 📚 Reading List
Save interesting blogs to read later.

### ❤️ Interaction
Show your appreciation for other users' content.

### 🔐 Security
Your account is protected with secure authentication.

## Get Started

Ready to start? You can:

- [View all blogs](/blogs) to discover content
- [Create your first blog](/blogs/new) and share your ideas
- [Sign up](/register) if you don't have an account yet
